## 🔴 Business Problem  

The restaurant industry is highly competitive, and platforms like Zomato generate **massive amounts of data daily**.  
Without structured analysis:  
- ❌ Restaurants can’t identify what drives ratings  
- ❌ Customers can’t compare value across cuisines/locations  
- ❌ Zomato can’t highlight top performers effectively  

➡️ The goal is to analyze Zomato data to uncover **key factors influencing restaurant success**.

---

## 🎯 Task (My Role as Data Analyst)

| Task | Objective |
|------|-----------|
| 🧹 **Data Cleaning** | Handle missing values, fix data types |
| 📊 **EDA** | Explore restaurant ratings across cuisines & locations |
| 🍽️ **Cuisine Analysis** | Identify cuisines with highest average ratings |
| 📍 **Location Analysis** | Compare performance by city/area |
| 💰 **Cost Analysis** | Study relationship between cost and ratings |
| 📈 **Visualization** | Build charts to communicate insights |

---

## ⚙️ Action (What I Did)

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║   🧹 Data Cleaning   |   📊 Exploratory Analysis   |   📈 Visualization ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝


- 🧹 **Data Cleaning** → Removed nulls, standardized cuisine/location names, fixed data types  
- 📊 **Exploratory Analysis** → Ratings distribution, cost vs rating correlation  
- 🍽️ **Cuisine Analysis** → Grouped by cuisine type to find top performers  
- 📍 **Location Analysis** → Compared average ratings across cities & neighborhoods  
- 📈 **Visualization** → Histograms, bar charts, heatmaps for restaurant success drivers  

---

## 📈 Results  

- ✅ **Cuisine Trends** → North Indian & Italian cuisines had highest average ratings  
- ✅ **Location Insights** → Restaurants in premium areas scored ~20% higher on average  
- ✅ **Cost vs Rating** → Moderate‑priced restaurants (₹300–₹800) had best ratings  
- ✅ **Rating Distribution** → Majority clustered between 3.5–4.5 stars  
- ✅ **Customer Preference** → Multi‑cuisine restaurants attracted more reviews
